Theme: **From blob to blueprint** — deep learning that turns cryo-EM density maps
into atomic models. The pipeline: find particles in noisy micrographs → sharpen the
reconstructed map → detect secondary structure → trace the protein backbone → build
and *identify* a full atomic model → and do the same for nucleic acids. Structure is
a load-bearing layer under function, drug discovery, and the virtual cell, and this
is the AI making experimental structure determination faster and more objective.

## Anchors (Europe PMC-verified; abstracts captured)

1. **Positive-unlabeled convolutional neural networks for particle picking in
   cryo-electron micrographs (Topaz)** — Bepler, Morin, ..., Berger. *Nature Methods*
   (2019). DOI 10.1038/s41592-019-0575-8 | PMID 31591578.
   Why: the first step. "Identifying a sufficient number of particles for analysis can
   take months of manual effort"; Topaz is "an efficient and accurate particle-picking
   pipeline using neural networks trained with a general-purpose positive-unlabeled
   learning method," which "retrieves many more real particles … while maintaining low
   false-positive rates," including "small, non-globular and asymmetric particles."

2. **DeepEMhancer: a deep learning solution for cryo-EM volume post-processing** —
   Sanchez-Garcia, Gomez-Blanco, ..., Vargas. *Communications Biology* (2021).
   DOI 10.1038/s42003-021-02399-1 | PMID 34267316.
   Why: sharpen the map. Because maps "generally need to be post-processed to improve
   their interpretability," DeepEMhancer, "trained on a dataset of pairs of
   experimental maps and maps sharpened using their respective atomic models," learned
   "masking-like and sharpening-like operations in a single step," reducing noise and
   revealing "more detailed versions of the experimental maps."

3. **Protein secondary structure detection in intermediate-resolution cryo-EM maps
   using deep learning (Emap2sec)** — Maddhuri Venkata Subramaniya, Terashi, Kihara.
   *Nature Methods* (2019). DOI 10.1038/s41592-019-0500-1 | PMID 31358979.
   Why: read the fold when resolution is poor. Emap2sec "uses a three-dimensional deep
   convolutional neural network to assign secondary structure to each grid point in an
   EM map" at "resolutions of between 5 and 10 Å," "showed substantially better
   performance than existing methods" — recovering structure where atoms aren't yet
   visible.

4. **DeepTracer for fast de novo cryo-EM protein structure modeling and special
   studies on CoV-related complexes** — Pfab, Phan, Si. *PNAS* (2021).
   DOI 10.1073/pnas.2017525118 | PMID 33361332.
   Why: automate backbone tracing. "A fully automated deep learning-based method for
   fast de novo multichain protein complex structure determination from high-resolution
   cryo-EM maps"; on 476 experimental maps "residue coverage increased by over 30% …
   and the rmsd value improved from 1.29 Å to 1.18 Å," with strong results on
   coronavirus complexes.

5. **Automated model building and protein identification in cryo-EM maps
   (ModelAngelo)** — Jamali, Kimanius, ..., Scheres (MRC LMB). *Nature* (2024).
   DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-07215-4 | PMID 38408488.
   Why: the flagship. Interpreting maps "requires high levels of expertise and
   labour-intensive manual intervention"; ModelAngelo "combines information from the
   cryo-EM map with information from protein sequence and structure in a single graph
   neural network," builds models "of similar quality to those generated by human
   experts," and — using "predicted amino acid probabilities … in hidden Markov model
   sequence searches" — "outperforms human experts in the identification of proteins
   with unknown sequences."

6. **CryoREAD: de novo structure modeling for nucleic acids in cryo-EM maps using
   deep learning** — Giri & Kihara (Kihara lab). *Nature Methods* (2023).
   DOI 10.1038/s41592-023-02032-5 | PMID 37783885.
   Why: extend it to DNA/RNA. "Computational methods for nucleic acid structure
   modeling are relatively scarce"; CryoREAD "identifies phosphate, sugar and base
   positions in a cryo-EM map using deep learning, which are traced and modeled into a
   three-dimensional structure," building "substantially more accurate models than
   existing methods" at 2.0–5.0 Å (with SARS-CoV-2 complexes as a testbed).

## Horizon radar (strategy note)
- This is **AI closing the loop on experimental structure determination** — the
  complement to AlphaFold's *prediction*. Prediction gives you a hypothesis; map
  interpretation gives you the ground truth, faster and more objectively (ModelAngelo
  even beats experts at protein identification).
- The pattern is the lab's own: **turn expert-and-labour bottlenecks into learned,
  automated, reusable tools** — the same thesis behind BioEngine and the model zoo,
  applied to structural biology.
- Structure feeds everything downstream the lab tracks: [function](/post/newsletter-2026-09-02/),
  [interactions](/post/newsletter-2026-09-26/), [drug binding](/post/newsletter-2026-09-24/),
  and the mechanistic layer of the virtual cell.

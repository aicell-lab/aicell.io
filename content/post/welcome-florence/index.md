We are delighted to welcome **Florence Stadelmann** to the AICell Lab as a research intern. Florence
is an M.Sc. student in Molecular Biotechnology at Uppsala University, and she joins us drawn by exactly
the mix that defines the lab: bioinformatics, scientific automation, and data-driven biology.

## What Florence will work on

Florence will focus on the **operation and validation of our automated live-cell imaging platform** —
the kind of work that keeps a self-driving lab honest. Day to day, that means running scheduled
multi-day imaging experiments, validating the platform's control software through fault testing and
automated test scripts, and supporting quantitative bioimage analysis. It's a role right at the seam
between the wet bench and the code that runs it — where reliable automation turns tedious, repetitive
routines into reproducible data.

## In her own words

We asked Florence a few questions as she settles in.

#### Q: What excites you most about working with lab automation?

> "Honestly, having a robot arm run the lab work is just insanely cool. I've always been curious about
> automating the repetitive lab bench routines, so you can skip the tedious parts and focus entirely on
> the data analysis and problem-solving."

#### Q: What are you most looking forward to during your internship?

> "I'm mostly super hyped to experience what it's actually like working inside an active research group
> — seeing the day-to-day collaboration up close and learning how the team tackles complex research
> roadblocks in real time."

#### Q: What drew you to molecular biotechnology and data science?

> "Scientific automation and data science always fascinated me even more than traditional wet lab
> biology. I wanted an education where I could apply data science directly to real-world challenges, and
> molecular biotech felt like the perfect route — especially since the pharma and biotech space is so
> exciting right now."

#### Q: A fun fact about you?

> "I used to compete for the Swedish National Team in Taekwondo (ITF) back in 2019!" 🥋

## A warm welcome

Florence's enthusiasm for automation and data-driven biology fits right in, and we're excited to have
her helping our imaging platform run reliably around the clock. Please join us in giving her a warm
welcome!
